## Local delivery-state checkpoint (2026-11-09)

Three clients now confirm read state by exact message ID rather than a sender-controlled timestamp watermark. Persistent per-message receipt jobs keep the event ID/timestamp stable, stage at most 64 per flush, retry exact already-sealed ciphertext and reschedule the highest known receipt on authenticated duplicate delivery. A thirty-second replay cooldown coalesces duplicates; receipt events do not create reciprocal ACK jobs. Existing V2 read history is backfilled when viewed without recreating cleared content. Foreground/window/latest-viewport gates remain in place.

The relay retains original root-signed cancellation proofs and supplies them in a negative key response or new `0x4C` submit rejection. All clients verify the purpose/signature and recipient root before treating that identity as permanently revoked. They stop its pending retries/calls, preserve local history and prior confirmed statuses, and show a small unconfirmed-delivery error. Unsigned relay metadata cannot establish this outcome; late server ACKs cannot mask it, while an authenticated peer receipt may still prove an earlier delivery of its exact message.
